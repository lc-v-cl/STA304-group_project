Question 4 (rating scale)
Question 4 uses a five-point scale from very bad to very good. Please extend it to 7 points, for example by adding "terrible" and "excellent" at the ends. A 7-point scale gives you more variation to work with in your analysis. (You can also optionally do a 10-point scale.)

Question 5 (hours of study)
Please ask respondents to enter the actual number of hours per week they spend studying instead of choosing a range. This is not a personal question, so a range does not protect anyone, and it costs you quantitative data that would improve your results.

Question 7 (number of courses)
Some students take only one or two courses, and others take seven, for example students who want to graduate quickly or who study very part-time. Please make sure your options do not lump these students together, so you do not lose that variation. Asking for the actual number of courses would work well.
